🚨 Real-World Consequences

  • The Time-Decay Trap: Cooked meals have a strict 2-hour ambient limit under FSSAI. Manual coordination takes 3+ hours, rendering food unsafe.
  • Cold-Chain Mismatch: Shelters receive 40 kg donations but only have 10 kg of spare refrigeration space, causing secondary spoilage.
  • Zero Operational Visibility: Donors cannot track whether food reached children; volunteers lack navigation.
  • Economic Disincentive: Discarding food into landfills is treated as a total loss rather than a tax-deductible contribution.

Operational Pipeline

How the Rescue Works

The 10-step deterministic state machine from surplus intake to verified drop-off.

1 Donor Intake: Donor enters food title, category, quantity, and preparation time.
2 Feasibility Validation: System enforces a minimum 3 kg threshold to justify courier dispatch emissions.
3 Location Authentication: Geocoded via OpenStreetMap Nominatim or verified GPS coordinates.
4 Proximity Matching: Haversine great-circle calculation identifies nearest compatible shelter with spare cold capacity.
5 Shelter Notification: Offer displayed with a 15-minute priority countdown timer.
6 Acceptance / Cascade: Shelter accepts; cold storage meter increments. (If passed, cascades to next shelter).
7 Courier Claim: Volunteer claims job from Available Pool (Status: 'volunteer_assigned').
8 GPS Routing: Interactive Leaflet map provides turn-by-turn navigation between bay and shelter.
9 Confirmed Drop-off: Recipient confirms receipt; volunteer receives verified mission badge.
10 Impact Logging: System aggregates CO2e avoided and issues an audit-ready Section 80G PDF receipt.

Technical Architecture

Smart Matching & Business Logic

Deterministic, rule-based matching based on geospatial distance, cold capacity, and dietary criteria.

📐 Haversine Geospatial Proximity

Calculates the exact great-circle distance between donor pickup bay and recipient shelters:

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ1)·cos(φ2)·sin²(Δλ/2)
d = 2 · R · atan2(√a, √(1-a))
(Earth radius R = 6371 km)

Executed against MongoDB 2dsphere index for sub-10ms query execution.

⚖️ Deterministic Eligibility Rules

  • Cold Storage Check: (shelter.capacityKg - shelter.currentStorageUsedKg) >= donation.quantityKg
  • Dietary Compliance: Pure Veg shelters only receive batches tagged Veg Only.
  • Perishability Window: Estimated travel time must be substantially less than remaining safe shelf life.
  • Technical Integrity Note: Implemented with transparent, audit-ready deterministic logic—strictly avoiding unverified neural predictions.

Operational Fault-Tolerance

Volunteer Vehicle Breakdown Recovery

A malfunction in transit does not destroy the food rescue mission.

🛵 Emergency Re-Dispatch State Machine

  1. Volunteer encounters mechanical issue (flat tyre, engine fault, battery drain).
  2. Taps "Report Malfunction" inside their active mission card.
  3. Selects incident cause and captures real-time breakdown GPS coordinates.
  4. Backend unassigns courier with zero penalty or rating loss.
  5. Job is flagged re_dispatch_needed and boosted to the top of the Available Pool.

🛡️ Two Handled Operational Scenarios

  • Breakdown Before Pickup: Volunteer has not received food yet. Mission is returned immediately to the pool for direct pickup at the donor bay.
  • Breakdown Mid-Transit (With Food): Courier is in possession of perishable food. System activates relay_needed; the breakdown coordinates become the new pickup point for an emergency courier handover.
  • Stakeholder Sync: Donors and receiving shelters receive updated ETAs automatically.

Access & Integrity

Security, Access Control & UI Integrity

Role isolation, geocoded input validation, and stacking-context resilient modals.

🛡️ RoleGuard & Scoped Navigation

  • Strict route barriers in RoleGuard.jsx intercept unauthorized URL navigation.
  • Donors only see 'Donate'; Shelters only see 'Receive'; Volunteers only see 'Volunteer Board'.
  • Prevents competitors or bad actors from inspecting food queues.

🗺️ Nominatim Geocode Authentication

  • Live verification against OpenStreetMap Nominatim API.
  • Fake/gibberish addresses (e.g. abc nagar, xyz road) are identified as 0 results and blocked.
  • Supports browser GPS reverse-geocoding for authenticated dispatch.

🚪 React Portal Architecture

  • Auth and Profile modals mounted via createPortal(..., document.body).
  • Explicit z-[9999] prevents clipping inside sticky headers or scrolling banners.
  • Tested and responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports.

User Guidance

MealBot: User Assistance & Website Guidance

An LLM-powered assistant reducing onboarding friction and explaining food safety regulations.

🤖 What MealBot Does

  • Explains Platform Features: Helps first-time donors and volunteers understand how to use the website.
  • FSSAI Safety Guidance: Informs users on 2-hour ambient holding rules, hot hold (>60°C), and cold storage (<5°C).
  • Section 80G Tax Education: Explains how commercial kitchens claim 50% tax deductions on food donations.
  • Speech Synthesis: Features browser-native Web Speech API voice narration for accessibility.

🚫 Clear Technical Boundary (What MealBot Is NOT)

  • NOT an operational dispatcher: Matching is strictly governed by deterministic backend logic.
  • NOT a decision-maker: It does not re-route couriers or allocate food batches.
  • NOT predictive ML: Expiry calculations use deterministic statutory thresholds (e.g. rice = 4 hrs).
  • Architectural Role: Strictly a natural-language guide to make complex civic software intuitive for everyday citizens.
